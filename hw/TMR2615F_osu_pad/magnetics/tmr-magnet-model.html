Method & assumptions

  • Field model: axially magnetized cylinder as an equivalent solenoid — a stack of current loops, each evaluated off-axis via complete elliptic integrals K, E (AGM). Verified against the analytic on-axis face-field of a Ø10×10 mm, Br = 1.3 T magnet: model 0.5814 T vs analytic 0.5814 T.
  • Board geometry (measured from TMR2615F_osu_pad.kicad_pcb): sensor 3.09 mm radially offset from switch center; all assembly on the F side, switch inserted from the B side — so the magnet sees the die through the 1.62 mm board. Sensing axis is in-plane, pointed through the switch axis (reads the radial field component).
  • Magnet data: Gateron's dimensioned 5:2 engineering drawings (hw/refs/switches/) were rendered at 600 dpi and pixel-measured (measure_switch.py), calibrated against two dimensioned base widths per view — every measured Gateron switch uses the same Ø2.77 × 3.41 mm cylinder; families differ in carry height and grade (implied Br 1.16–1.66 T). Remanence is solved from the published bottom-out flux; the published initial flux (quoted at 0.3 mm pressed) is a held-out check, matching within 2–14 %. Switches without drawings (KS-20/Lekker, KS-37B, TTC, Kailh, Akko) assume the measured magnet with Br 1.3 T and a fitted carry height — treat those as ±25 %. Everglide Sticky Rice V2 (120 → 800 Gs, 3.5 mm) models like a shorter-travel KS-37B; see fit_switches.py for the full 12-row table.
  • Sensor: TMR2615F-AAC-1.500-500 — ±500 Gs magnetic linear range, 1.5 mV/V/Gs, mid-rail offset; usable output 5–95 % VDD ⇒ electrical rails at ±300 Gs (ratiometric, VDD-independent). Output direction depends on magnet polarity: every modern Gateron drawing states "Magnet N-pole is facing down" (despite community lore calling Gateron S-down); the bipolar TMR2615F handles either, but don't mix polarities on one board unless firmware calibrates sign per key.
  • Spec provenance: Wooting publishes no flux figures — Lekker (*) is modeled as its manufacturer-sibling Gateron KS-20 (community-assumed, not vendor-confirmed). Akko figures are their 1.6 mm-PCB spec. Kailh Flame/Mistral ("Source" series) quote a 2000 Gs "trigger flux" at an unknown measurement point — unreachable through 1.9 mm by any plausible stem magnet, so they're excluded here; bench-test before using them. Raesha (DrunkDeer) and Geon Raptor HE publish no flux at all. KS-37B/Fox, KS-20, TTC and Kailh Magnetic God never state polarity ("n/p").
  • What to verify on hardware: log the ADC across full travel with one real switch — one bench measurement collapses all magnet-fit uncertainty.
