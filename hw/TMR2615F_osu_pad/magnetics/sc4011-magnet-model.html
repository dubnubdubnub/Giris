Swapping the TMR2615F for a Semiment SC4011 (DFN1616)

The SC4011 is a planar Hall sensor: it reads Bz, perpendicular to the board, where the TMR2615F reads Bx in the board plane. The sensor site doesn't move — only which field component it sees. Same validated cylinder-magnet model as the TMR page, recomputed on the axial component, with the SC4011's real output limits and the board's actual signal chain.

The Hall plate sits ~0.30 mm below the board's F-side surface — the same depth as the TMR die, so the magnet-to-sensor gap is unchanged. What changes is the component: at r = 3.09 mm the axial field is roughly half the radial field's swing, and it falls off far faster with radial error. Resolution is quoted against a 12-bit ADC on the 3.3 V VDDA reference (0.806 mV/LSB), single conversion, no oversampling.

Footprint merge — the two land patterns share a grid

Both parts are 1.6 × 1.6 mm with 0.5 mm pad pitch in X and pin 1 at bottom-left in top view. Pad rows sit at y = ±0.62 mm (TMR2615F) vs ±0.575 mm (SC4011) — a 0.045 mm difference, easily absorbed by one shared land. A merged 6-pad pattern plus the TMR's centre bar carries both parts.

Net cost: three solder jumpers per key (VCC/SIG selector, VDD gate, OUT tap) — 18 for the six keys. Rotating the SC4011 doesn't help: at 180° its OUT lands on the TMR's GND pad. Since the SC4011 is Z-axis, its rotation about the board normal is magnetically free, so orientation is purely a routing choice — unlike the TMR, which must stay aimed at the switch axis.

Method & assumptions

  • Field model: unchanged from the TMR page — axially magnetized cylinder as an equivalent solenoid, stack of current loops, off-axis via complete elliptic integrals K, E (AGM), verified against the analytic on-axis face field of a Ø10×10 mm Br = 1.3 T magnet. This page simply reads the axial component Bz out of the same solver instead of the radial Bx.
  • Board geometry (measured from TMR2615F_osu_pad.kicad_pcb): sensor 3.09 mm radially offset from the switch centre, assembly on F, switch inserted from B, magnet sees the sensor through the 1.62 mm board. The Ø4 mm centre NPTH is why the sensor is off-axis at all — there is no on-axis position available for a Z-axis part.
  • Hall plate depth is an estimate, not a datasheet figure. Semiment does not publish the plate's location within the DFN1616 body. 0.30 mm below the F-side surface assumes the die sits on the 0.127 mm lead frame with the plate at the top of a ~0.17 mm die — the same depth the TMR2615F specifies. Its in-plane position is also unpublished, so the effective r carries roughly ±0.2 mm of uncertainty, which at this offset is worth about ±20 % of Bz swing. Both are sliders here for that reason.
  • SC4011 model: VQ = VCC/2; output floor 0.8 V and ceiling VCC−0.7 V per §12; sensitivity interpolated linearly between the two specified points (1.6 mV/Gs at 3.3 V, 2.9 mV/Gs at 5.0 V — note these are not proportional to VCC, so this is an interpolation, not a ratiometric law). Min/max bands interpolate the 1.2/2.0 and 2.3/3.5 mV/Gs limits the same way.
  • Polarity: for the DN package an S pole toward the marked face is the positive direction. The sensor's marked face points away from the magnet on this board, so N-down Gateron switches drive the output one way and S-down the other. Either fits — the part is bipolar around VQ and the field never approaches the clamps. Sign is a firmware concern, not a hardware one.
  • Placement sensitivity is the argument against this swap that the charts don't show directly: near r = 3.09 mm the Bx swing degrades about 3.4 %/0.1 mm of radial error, Bz about 9.8 %/0.1 mm, and the Bz swing crosses zero entirely at r ≈ 4.4 mm. Drag the offset slider to see it.
  • What to verify on hardware: the SC4011's unspecified output noise, measured on this board through this mux at this scan rate. Everything else here is bounded by datasheet numbers; that one is not, and it decides the swap.
