  6. Output Characteristics
  As shown in Figure 5, the output voltage of the TMR2615x-AAC IC changes with the external magnetic field
  intensity. When there is no magnetic field, VOUT outputs 50% VDD, and when the magnetic field changes from
  -B to B, the linear output voltage range of VOUT for TMR2615D-AAC is 5% VDD to 95% VDD, the linear output
  voltage range of VOUT for TMR2615F-AAC is 95% VDD to 5% VDD. Customers can modify the linear range
  through the adjustment provided by the TMR2615x-AAC.
